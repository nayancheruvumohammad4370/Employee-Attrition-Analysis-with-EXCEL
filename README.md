# Employee Attrition Analysis Using Excel
This project focuses on analyzing employee attrition trends to understand the key factors influencing workforce turnover. Using Excel dashboards, visualizations, and calculated metrics, the analysis highlights patterns across demographics, compensation, job roles, satisfaction levels, and work experience.

# Objective
To identify the major drivers of employee attrition and provide actionable insights that can help organizations improve retention strategies and workplace engagement.

# Dataset Overview
The dataset includes key HR attributes such as:
Department & Job Role
Age & Gender
Education Level
Compensation & Salary Hike
Job & Environment Satisfaction
Distance from Home & Tenure
Performance Rating & Overtime

# Key Insights from the Analysis
Higher attrition is observed among:
Younger employees
Technical and sales roles
Employees with low satisfaction scores
Lower salary or limited career growth opportunities
Longer commute distances and overtime also show impact on employee exit behavior.

# Tools Used
Microsoft Excel
Data Cleaning
Pivot Tables
Charts & Visualizations
Slicers & Filtering

# Output
The final output includes:
Interactive Excel Dashboard
Attrition patterns across demographics, compensation, satisfaction, and job role
Summary insights for reporting and decision-making

# Conclusion
Understanding attrition is crucial to improving employee experience and reducing hiring and training costs. Data-driven insights provide clarity on retention challenges and support informed HR strategies.
